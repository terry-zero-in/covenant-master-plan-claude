## D-2 — Queue ranking logic (Inbox / your-move ordering)

- **Status:** OPEN by prior ruling (date/risk/amount/staleness undefined).
- **Recommendation:** default order = **deadline proximity, then blocking severity** (certify-ready before exceptions when both are due same-day), with saved-view sort overrides. Rationale: the terminal risk is a late or wrong package; date-first matches Karbon/Vanta evidence; risk-first ordering is a lender-side instinct (superseded framing).
- **Alternative:** severity-first (shortfall/breach findings always top) — defensible for portfolios in distress.
- **Consequence:** W3 ships with the recommendation behind a config; changing later is a sort-order change, not a rebuild.

## D-3 — Calendar month grid

- **Status:** OPEN by prior ruling (task-first agenda liked; month-grid question undecided).
- **Recommendation:** agenda stays the only primary; add an optional **due-date-only compact heat-strip** (density dots, click scrolls the agenda) and no full month grid. Evidence: deadline clustering (every loan's Q+45 lands the same fortnight) makes day-cell grids truncate exactly at crunch (the "+N more" failure); the best compliance products render deadlines as annotated lists.
- **Alternative:** ship a secondary month view, due-date-only plotting, never the landing view (the TaxDome concession pattern).
- **Consequence:** none blocking; the heat-strip is additive.

## D-7 — Intake auto-file confidence posture

- **Status:** The automation dial (file quietly vs queue for human) needs an initial default; evidence supports the mechanism, not the number.

## D-8 — Email transport timing for send

- **Consequence:** copy in the send surface says "download and deliver" until transport ships; the flow vision's "automatically sends" is realized in the follow-up.
